About me

Aiman Nurdeeni

I'm a UX designer with a research-led, systems-minded approach to digital design — from lo-fi wireframes through to shipped front-end code. I studied at SUTD [BEng Computer Science & Design, 2022], and I've since worked across product design, government-facing tools, and — since June 2024 — as an IT Trainer at Pathlight School, designing accessible digital skills curriculum for students and adults with special needs. Applying UX principles and design thinking to real classrooms has sharpened how I think about accessibility and designing for genuine constraints, not just personas.

Experience

  • Jun 2024 – Present

    IT Trainer — Pathlight School

    Design and deliver accessible digital skills curriculum for students and adults with special needs, covering coding, graphic design, web design, animation, 3D modelling and AI tools. Apply UX principles and design thinking to create inclusive learning experiences tailored to diverse cognitive and learning profiles.

    Built an online learning platform on Google Sites as a UI-optimised repository of bite-sized digital skills lessons. Lead the school's primary STEM club through the full design thinking process — user research, ideation, prototyping, and testing physical and digital solutions. Run staff upskilling workshops on digital tools for non-technical educators, and iterate curriculum based on continuous user feedback and learner outcomes. Collaborate with a team of IT Trainers to scale digital literacy programmes across student and adult learner populations.

  • 2022 – 2023

    UI/UX Designer — OrangeKloud

    Contributed to the UI/UX enhancement of the company's digital services and apps using Figma and the Adobe Suite. Helped standardise design guidelines and define work processes across the design and development teams.

  • 2021

    UX Design Intern — Singapore Police Force, Ops-Tech Department

    Contributed to UX research and design for internal mobile applications. Conducted user interviews and refined Figma prototypes, applying industry design-thinking tools such as Journey Mapping and the Rose-Thorn-Bud framework to frame problems.
